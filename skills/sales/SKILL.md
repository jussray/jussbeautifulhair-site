# /sales — Public Hair

Use for public positioning, merchandising, offer presentation, conversion, checkout messaging, retention, and customer-care planning.

## Required sequence

1. Complete 5W1H: customer, owner, offer, surface, timing, reason, and authorized action.
2. Inspect exact catalog, approved prices, stock signals, proof, checkout boundary, accessibility, fulfillment, and support.
3. Qualify customer fit and define disqualifiers.
4. Build value from verified Story, Quality, Care, and Proof without exposing private operations.
5. Test total cost, shipping, returns, care expectations, checkout friction, and mobile/accessibility behavior.
6. Answer objections only from approved public facts.
7. Propose the smallest reversible commitment. No approval carries forward.
8. Measure conversion quality, checkout completion, complaints/refunds, fulfillment, support burden, repeat purchase, and retained trust.

## Boundaries

- Never expose vendors, costs, negotiations, private admin logic, customer exports, credentials, or strategy.
- Never invent origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, stock, scarcity, testimonials, savings, delivery, returns, or quality proof.
- Do not change prices, discounts, catalog, checkout, analytics, claims, communications, domains, deployment, or customer data without the exact founder gate.
- Keep Untold Stories products, customers, checkout, suppliers, and fulfillment outside the hair storefront.
- Use respectful, age-appropriate messaging and never use sensitive personal information for persuasion.
- No dark patterns, deceptive urgency, hidden fees, or client-authoritative payment logic.

## Output

Return customer, problem, fit and disqualifiers, offer, proof, assumptions, objections, checkout and support implications, measurement, rollback, and next approval gate.

A sales plan is not authorization.
