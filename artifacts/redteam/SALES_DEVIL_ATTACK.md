# Public Hair Sales Red Team

Premise risks: assumed demand, unsupported product claims, conversion-first thinking, checkout friction, inaccessible presentation, stale price or stock information, and leakage of private vendor or customer data.

Plan risks: deceptive urgency, discount dependence, second checkout or deployment authority, client-authoritative payment state, misleading quality language, Untold catalog contamination, and measuring clicks while refunds, support burden, or trust worsen.

Controls: approved public facts, explicit disqualifiers, accessible mobile proof, exact checkout boundary, truthful total-cost and fulfillment expectations, separate gates for pricing, claims, analytics, communication, deployment, and rollback.

Verdict: decision support only; `/devil` reviews material public commercial changes.
