# JBH Public Storefront Repair Authority

This repository owns the public Juss Beautiful Hair storefront experience. It follows the portfolio necessary-fix default while preserving Shopify as catalog/cart/checkout authority and keeping customer/payment/business mutations separately gated.

## Trusted repair authority

A trusted repository operator may repair source, tests, CI, storefront routes/components, non-secret integration configuration, release verification, documentation, and the existing Cloudflare storefront runtime when the repair is bounded and reversible.

Every consequential repair must bind to the canonical repository/base/head, verified JBH store fingerprint, Cloudflare project/Worker/runtime identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, before state, rollback, and post-action readback. Identity movement expires inherited proof.

## Cloudflare storefront repairs

A trusted provider executor may restore the already-approved public storefront topology or deploy an already-authorized exact release when:

- the canonical JBH store and Cloudflare runtime fingerprints match;
- no credential rotation/exposure, new spend, ownership transfer, destructive deletion, or new domain ownership change is required;
- the operation is reversible and bounded to the failed surface;
- the previous runtime identity is retained for rollback;
- `/version` or equivalent immutable release identity is verified after deployment;
- desktop/mobile Playwright proves the affected storefront/cart path.

Cloudflare green is not Shopify catalog, checkout, payment, order, or fulfillment proof.

## Shopify boundary

The storefront may repair read/display/handoff defects while preserving current Shopify truth. Explicit founder/business authority remains required for product creation/removal, pricing/discounts, inventory, shipping/returns, checkout/payment behavior, order/customer mutation, vendor/fulfillment actions, app scopes, spending/billing, credentials, ownership transfer, and external customer/vendor communication outside approved automation.

## Record witnesses

Linear/Asana may repair task state. Notion/Drive/docs may repair stale requirements/runbooks. Slack may repair internal incident/status records. Gmail may provide customer/vendor consequence evidence. None gains store, deployment, payment, customer, or fulfillment authority merely by witnessing a problem.

Playwright is the authoritative witness for observable storefront behavior and must verify UI/runtime repairs; browser sessions are never business authority.

Fingerprints/proof cookies are evidence-only. For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
