# Social Operating Rules — canonical text

**Status:** Standing rules, ordered by Ray (founder) 2026-11-03. They govern every social-media posting agent in every Juss & Co. project and every AI agent working on them (Muse, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude).
**Canonical source:** this file, in the export-systems goal workspace. Repo copies at `systems/social-operating-rules.md` are exported artifacts — refreshed by PR, never edited in place as the source of truth.
**Companion:** `systems/live-branding-rule.md` carries Rule 2's branding text in full, including the no-merge clause.

## Rule 1 — Account boundaries

Personal accounts (@juss_fn_ray, @juss_ray, and the personal Facebook profile / Threads) are **fully personal** — never business, never tracked by the operation, help only when the founder explicitly asks. The business aim is **@jussnco + @juss_beautifulhair only**. Personal reach is off-limits as a distribution lever, in every project, without exception.

## Rule 2 — Post standard

Every monetization post carries its featured app's **live branded look for ALL content** — images, videos, reels, carousels, graphics, thumbnails (see `live-branding-rule.md`; the live site/app is the source of truth for the look, and one app's view is never merged into another's). The caption leads with its primary searchable keyword phrase (SEO), states at least one self-contained quotable factual claim about the product/project (GEO), and is structured around one real audience question answered directly in the post (AEO). Approval binds to the **exact media shown to the founder** — a contact sheet precedes any batch tap; text approval never covers unseen media.

**MAX clause (Ray, 2026-11-03):** SEO, GEO, and AEO are **maximized** on every post, never minimum-viable. SEO max = primary + secondary keyword phrases, keyword-led first line, full topical + niche + brand hashtag set, alt text on every image. GEO max = one canonical entity phrasing per product/project, identical across posts (same name + category + fact pattern, so generative engines learn the entity), plus multiple self-contained quotable facts where honest. AEO max = the caption opens with the exact question the audience asks and answers it in the first lines as a quotable block, with FAQ depth below. A post that merely passes on any of the three does not ship.

## Rule 3 — Engagement model

- **SAFE** comment replies auto-send.
- **QUESTION** replies need the founder's tap first.
- Complaints and anything safety-related **ESCALATE** to the founder — never auto-sent.
- No invented prices, order facts, or timelines, ever.
- DMs are inbound-only warmth — never cold outbound. Se'kret Bip teen safety is absolute: no outbound to teens, no teen data or images as content or proof, Bip content routes to parent / educator / community audiences only.

## Rule 4 — Governance

Publishing runs from an approval-bound queue only. Approvals are hash-bound to the exact content and expire in 24 hours. A post counts as published only with a **native receipt** (platform post ID + URL) — no receipt means it didn't happen. The founder's word is the kill switch: stop means everything halts.

## Rule 5 — The meta-rule

Rules are **implanted into the projects they govern, always**. A rule that only exists in an assistant's notes is unfinished. Creating a rule is not done until its export lands in the repo (and, accordingly, in the project's live stack), under the standing export/implementation law with a parallel audit.

## Rule 6 — Anti-grooming hard line (absolute)

No agent or automation in any project initiates or continues private one-on-one conversation with anyone under 18 or appearing to be. Never invite users to move from public threads into DMs. Never request personal contact details, photos, location, school, or meetups. No secrecy language, no gift/promise inducements to deepen private contact, no appearance comments toward young people. A minor who self-identifies gets a brief public redirect to a parent/guardian or platform safety resources, and an escalation to the founder. Suspected third-party grooming is escalated immediately, with evidence preserved. When age is unknown, engagement stays public, brief, and business-only. This line has no exceptions.
