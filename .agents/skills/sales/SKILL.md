# /sales — Juss Beautiful Hair public storefront skill

Use `/sales` for public positioning, merchandising, offer presentation, conversion, checkout messaging, retention, and customer-care planning.

## Required flow

1. Complete 5W1H: customer, decision owner, offer, public surface, timing, reason, and authorized action.
2. Inspect the exact public catalog, approved prices, inventory signals, product proof, checkout boundary, analytics evidence, accessibility, fulfillment promises, and support path.
3. Qualify customer fit and define disqualifiers.
4. Build value from verified Story, Quality, Care, and Proof without exposing private sourcing or operations.
5. Test offer clarity, total cost, shipping, returns, care expectations, checkout friction, and mobile/accessibility behavior.
6. Answer objections only from approved public facts.
7. Propose the smallest reversible next commitment.
8. Measure qualified conversion, checkout completion, refunds/complaints, fulfillment quality, support burden, repeat purchase, and retained trust.

## Public boundaries

- Never expose vendor names, costs, sourcing negotiations, private admin logic, customer/order exports, owner credentials, or private strategy.
- Never invent origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, stock, scarcity, testimonials, savings, delivery, return eligibility, or quality proof.
- Do not change prices, discounts, catalog, checkout, analytics, claims, communications, domains, deployment, or customer data without the exact founder gate.
- Keep Untold Stories products, customers, checkout, suppliers, and fulfillment outside the hair storefront.
- Do not target minors or use sensitive traits or private customer content for persuasion.
- No dark patterns, deceptive urgency, hidden fees, or client-authoritative payment logic.

## Output

Return customer, problem, fit/disqualifiers, offer, public proof, assumptions, objections, checkout/support implications, measurement, rollback, and next approval gate. A draft is not authorization.
