# Lindy Sales Durability — Public Hair

Durable public selling depends on clear product facts, useful care guidance, honest total cost, reliable checkout, accessible mobile presentation, fulfillment reliability, support, and repeat trust.

Keep commerce, analytics, deployment, and model providers replaceable. Preserve portable offer versions, public product facts, consent-aware aggregate metrics, and reversible storefront changes.

Do not build the moat from one social channel, ad platform, model, checkout provider, or temporary tactic. The durable moat is Story + Quality + Care + Proof delivered consistently without exposing private operations.
