# /devil — Juss Beautiful Hair public storefront adversarial skill

Use `/devil` before material merchandising, pricing, discount, checkout, analytics, claim, campaign, customer-communication, domain, or deployment decisions.

## Pass I — attack the premise

- Is the customer problem and demand verified on the intended public surface?
- Is the offer distinct, understandable, deliverable, and appropriate for the hair catalog?
- Are product facts, prices, stock, shipping, returns, care, and support current?
- Is a storefront change necessary, or can the problem be solved with clearer approved information?
- Would the plan expose private vendors, costs, admin logic, customer/order data, credentials, or Untold Stories operations?

## Pass II — attack the plan

- Could the copy be technically true but materially misleading?
- What happens under wrong stock, price mismatch, checkout outage, payment failure, delayed fulfillment, refund, mobile failure, or accessibility barrier?
- Can conversion improve while trust, margin, support, or refund quality deteriorates?
- Does the plan create a second checkout, deployment, database, or customer-data authority?
- What are the kill criteria and rollback path?

## Output

Return counter-thesis, assumptions/evidence, failure and abuse cases, customer harm, privacy/catalog/deployment risks, commercial downside, kill criteria, minimum controls, rollback, and verdict.

A passing review does not authorize pricing, publication, outreach, checkout, data mutation, deployment, or merge.
