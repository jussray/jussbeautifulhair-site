# OODA Sales Execution Loop

**Observe:** exact catalog, approved product facts, price and stock signals, public proof, checkout, mobile accessibility, fulfillment promises, support, complaints, refunds, repeat purchase, consent-aware analytics, and deployment boundaries.

**Orient:** complete 5W1H; separate known, inferred, assumed, unknown, blocked, and need-to-verify evidence; apply `/devil`, lindymode, L99 authority, redteam, and project separation.

**Decide:** choose one reversible public hypothesis with success, failure, kill criteria, owner, evidence deadline, rollback, and founder approval gate.

**Act:** execute only the specifically approved step. Analysis does not authorize pricing, claims, communication, analytics, checkout, deployment, customer-data mutation, refunds, or publication.

**Loop:** record conversion quality, checkout completion, accessibility, fulfillment, support, refunds, complaints, and retention; continue, revise, pause, or stop from current proof.
