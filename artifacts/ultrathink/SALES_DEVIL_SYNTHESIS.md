# ULTRATHINK — Public Hair Sales + Devil

The public storefront should maximize the probability that a right-fit customer understands the product, total commitment, care expectations, checkout path, and support before paying.

`/sales` constructs qualification, merchandising, offer clarity, proof, checkout support, and retention. `/devil` attacks misleading claims, false urgency, stale catalog state, accessibility failure, checkout mismatch, support externalities, privacy leakage, and second-authority drift.

Operating chain:

`5W1H → public evidence → qualification → premise attack → durable offer → L99 authority → plan attack → founder gate → smallest storefront action → browser/runtime evidence → OODA`

Conversion is not success when refund, complaint, support, accessibility, margin, or trust quality deteriorates. Private sourcing and customer operations remain outside the public repository.
