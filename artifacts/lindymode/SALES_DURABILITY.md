# Lindy Sales Durability

Durable public selling depends on clear product facts, useful care guidance, honest total cost, reliable checkout, accessible mobile presentation, fulfillment reliability, support, and repeat trust.

Keep commerce, analytics, deployment, and model providers replaceable. Preserve portable offer versions, approved public facts, consent-aware aggregate measurements, proof, rollback, and founder approval.

Do not build the moat from one social channel, ad platform, model, checkout provider, temporary tactic, or deceptive urgency. The durable moat is Story + Quality + Care + Proof delivered consistently without exposing private operations.
