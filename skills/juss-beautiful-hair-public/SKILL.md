# Juss Beautiful Hair Public Storefront Skill

## Who

For AI operators working in the public storefront. The founder owns final product, pricing, claim, checkout, communication, domain, deployment, credential, and merge decisions.

## What

Use for public product presentation, accessibility, care guidance, approved proof, checkout handoff, deployment boundaries, brand moat, and exact-head verification.

## When

Invoke for storefront code, catalog presentation, public copy, customer-facing flows, analytics, checkout, browser proof, deployment configuration, or the hair moat.

## Where

- `client/` contains the public storefront.
- `brand/BRAND_MOAT.md` defines Story, Quality, Care, Proof, and product separation.
- `skills/` defines AI behavior.
- private vendors, costs, sourcing, admin, and customer operations belong in `jbh-private`.

## Why

The storefront must communicate a distinct hair offer without exposing private operations or creating a second payment, database, deployment, or customer-data authority.

## How

Use `/garyvee lindymode redteam l99 redteam ooda`. Complete 5W1H, attack the premise, prefer durable provider-neutral contracts, map authority and provenance, attack the selected plan, act minimally, verify desktop and mobile behavior, and loop.

## Product and data boundary

Juss Beautiful Hair retains hair products, customers, checkout, and fulfillment. Untold Stories products, vendors, customers, checkout, and fulfillment must not enter this catalog or public data. Private vendor identities, costs, negotiations, credentials, customer exports, and admin logic must not enter the public repository.

## Authority

Separate founder approval is required for catalog, price, discount, claims, communications, analytics, checkout/payment, customer data, credentials, domains, DNS, deployment, merge, refund, and rollback.

## Evidence

Run the applicable exact-head typecheck, lint, unit, deployment-boundary, build, AI-skill, and Playwright checks. Skipped, stale, transferred, missing-log, `steps: null`, or truncated-critical results are not proof. Browser proof must cover desktop, mobile, the existing hair catalog, and negative Untold-product leakage.

## Failure and rollback

Fail closed on uncertain product facts, private-data exposure, checkout or deployment drift, accessibility failure, catalog contamination, unsupported claims, or missing exact-head proof. Roll back through the exact commit, configuration, offer, or deployment change; never delete evidence.

## Ten-year maintenance contract

Version breaking authority, catalog, evidence, checkout, deployment, or skill changes. Keep providers replaceable, data portable, changes reversible, and reviews quarterly. Preserve a controlled upgrade path; do not promise zero maintenance.

## Definition of done

Complete only when exact-head checks execute and pass, public/private and hair/Untold boundaries remain intact, founder-gated actions remain pending or explicitly approved, rollback is documented, and remaining risks are zero or founder-accepted.
