# Public Hair Sales Red Team

Premise risks: assumed demand, unsupported product claims, conversion-first thinking, hidden checkout friction, inaccessible presentation, stale price/stock/shipping information, and leakage of private vendor or customer data.

Plan risks: deceptive urgency, discount dependence, second checkout or deployment authority, client-authoritative payment state, misleading quality/origin language, catalog contamination from Untold Stories, and measuring clicks while refunds, support burden, or trust worsen.

Required controls: approved public facts, explicit disqualifiers, accessible/mobile proof, exact checkout boundary, truthful total-cost and fulfillment expectations, separate gates for pricing/discounts/claims/analytics/communication/deployment, kill criteria, and rollback.

Verdict: `/sales` is decision support; `/devil` is mandatory before material public commercial changes.
