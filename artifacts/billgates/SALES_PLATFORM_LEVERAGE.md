# Bill Gates Artifact: Public Hair Sales Leverage

Treat the storefront as a reusable public offer interface: versioned product facts, story, care guidance, proof, total-cost expectations, checkout handoff, support, accessibility, outcome measurement, and rollback.

The public layer consumes only approved data from private operations. It never receives vendor identities, costs, sourcing negotiations, owner credentials, customer exports, or private strategy.

Keep commerce, analytics, deployment, and model providers behind replaceable boundaries. Every material offer or checkout change carries an exact version, accountable owner, evidence packet, approval, measurement plan, kill criteria, and rollback.

Ten-year leverage comes from a clear catalog contract, accessible presentation, truthful proof, reliable checkout and fulfillment handoff, retained customer trust, and knowledge that compounds without hidden state.
