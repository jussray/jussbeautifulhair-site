# Public Hair Sales Red Team

## Premise attack

Do not assume demand, differentiation, product fit, stock, delivery, repeat purchase, or customer trust. Require approved evidence for every public claim and identify customer disqualifiers before constructing an offer.

## Plan attack

Reject deceptive urgency, unsupported quality language, hidden fees, inaccessible presentation, stale catalog state, checkout confusion, client-authoritative payment state, private-data leakage, Untold Stories contamination, and execution without founder approval.

## Kill criteria

Stop when approved proof is missing, checkout or fulfillment truth is ambiguous, privacy or accessibility regresses, support cannot meet the promise, or the measured customer outcome contradicts the offer.

A passing redteam review does not authorize publication, pricing, checkout, deployment, communication, or customer-data use.
