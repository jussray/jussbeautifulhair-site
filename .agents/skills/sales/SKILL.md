# /sales

## Trigger

Use for public positioning, merchandising, offer clarity, customer qualification, checkout support, conversion analysis, and retention planning in `jussray/jussbeautifulhair-site`.

## Contract

Before recommending a public offer, establish:

- the exact customer and use case;
- qualification criteria and explicit disqualifiers;
- approved catalog, price, stock, policy, care, and fulfillment facts;
- the proof available for each claim;
- checkout, support, accessibility, privacy, and retention implications;
- the founder gate required before execution.

A sales plan is analysis, **not authorization**. It does not authorize product, price, discount, scarcity, testimonial, delivery, policy, analytics, communication, checkout, deployment, refund, customer-data, or publication changes.

## Proof rules

Never invent origin, grade, texture, construction, stock, demand, savings, delivery, testimonial, return eligibility, or customer outcomes. Separate known, inferred, assumed, unknown, blocked, and need-to-verify evidence.

Keep Juss Beautiful Hair separate from Untold Stories in products, suppliers, customers, checkout, fulfillment, and claims. Keep private vendors, costs, sourcing negotiations, customer exports, credentials, and strategy outside this repository.

## Output

Return the qualified customer, disqualifiers, approved proof, offer hypothesis, checkout and support impact, measurement plan, rollback, unresolved risk, and next founder approval gate.
