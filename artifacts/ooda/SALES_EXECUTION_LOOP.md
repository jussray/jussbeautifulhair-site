# OODA Sales Loop — Public Hair

**Observe:** exact catalog, approved product facts, price and stock signals, public proof, checkout, mobile and accessibility, fulfillment promises, support, complaints and refunds, repeat purchase, analytics consent, and deployment boundary.

**Orient:** complete 5W1H; separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns; apply `/devil`, Lindy durability, L99 authority, and hair/Untold separation.

**Decide:** choose one reversible public hypothesis with success, failure, kill criteria, owner, evidence deadline, and rollback.

**Act:** execute only the specifically approved step. Analysis does not authorize pricing, claims, analytics, communication, checkout, deployment, or customer-data mutation.

**Loop:** record conversion quality, checkout completion, accessibility, fulfillment, support, refunds, complaints, and retention; continue, revise, pause, or stop based on current proof.
