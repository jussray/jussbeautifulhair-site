# Bill Gates Artifact — Public Hair Sales Leverage

Treat the storefront as a reusable public offer interface: versioned product facts, story, care guidance, proof, total-cost expectations, checkout handoff, support path, accessibility, and outcome measurement.

The public layer may consume only approved data from private operations. It must never receive vendor identities, costs, sourcing negotiations, owner credentials, customer exports, or private strategy.

Provider-specific commerce, analytics, deployment, and model systems remain behind replaceable adapters. Every material offer or checkout change carries an exact version, evidence packet, approval, measurement plan, and rollback.

Ten-year leverage comes from a clear catalog contract, accessible presentation, truthful proof, reliable checkout/fulfillment handoff, and retained customer trust—not dependence on one platform, channel, or model.
