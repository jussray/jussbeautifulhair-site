# Juss Beautiful Hair Vision

Juss Beautiful Hair is a trustworthy, polished online storefront that makes premium hair and beauty products easy to understand, purchase, and receive without hiding price, shipping, payment, or support consequences.

## North star

Turn customer attention into repeat trust by making every product promise, price, checkout step, order handoff, and support path clear and verifiable.

## Product promises

1. **Customer truth before conversion tricks.** Product details, availability, price, shipping, returns, imagery, and claims match approved business reality.
2. **Secure payment boundaries.** The browser sends order intent to the server and redirects only to approved HTTPS Stripe checkout hosts.
3. **Public storefront and private operations stay separate.** Supplier terms, credentials, customer records, administrative tools, and webhook secrets do not enter the public bundle.
4. **Mobile customers receive a complete experience.** Navigation, product discovery, cart, checkout, policies, and support remain usable on phone-sized screens.
5. **Commerce states remain distinct.** A page view, cart, checkout session, payment, notification, fulfillment, shipment, refund, and completed order are not treated as the same event.

## Success condition

A customer can confidently answer what they are buying, what it costs, how payment works, when and where it ships, how to get help, and what happens if something goes wrong.
